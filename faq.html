❓Requirements for every dog we care for

Good Questions

General requirements and answers that apply to every dog. Questions about a specific service are answered on that service's own page.

What vaccinations are required?+

All dogs must be current on rabies and distemper, administered and documented by a licensed veterinarian. We suggest puppies receive at least one Bordetella vaccination because of their young immune systems. Please don't over-vaccinate — we absolutely accept titers for distemper.

What about flea and tick treatment?+

We expect every dog to be flea and tick free. If we find any, the bath and removal could cost up to $80 depending on severity and the size of the dog.

Is neuter or spay required?+

Yes — dogs must be neutered or spayed by 2 years of age. Female dogs cannot be in season (in heat) while at Happy Pets Palace. All dogs must be suitable for co-ed group play.

Can we tour your facility?+

Yes! We LOVE to give tours — stop by anytime we're open. We suggest touring any facility before leaving your dog. Here's what to look for:

  • You can tour every area without advance notice during business hours.
  • It's odor free and looks and feels clean.
  • Staff can answer your daycare or boarding questions and are kind toward the dogs.
  • Ask for references or check Google and Facebook reviews.
  • Vaccinations from a licensed vet are required before boarding.
  • Kennel suites are spacious and clean, with no chain-link that would allow fence fighting.
What is involved in your pre-screening process?+

After a quick phone or in-person chat about your dog's age, breed and dog-park experience, we do a 5-step evaluation on their first day of daycare:

  • Step 1 — Say hello: we see how your dog is with staff and being guided by the collar. Shy and timid dogs are welcome; we give them time.
  • Step 2 — Leash intros: one dog of each sex, one at a time, on leash.
  • Step 3 — Join the group: outside to sniff and potty, then we gradually build the group.
  • Step 4 — Pack feedback: we respect our regulars' read on a new dog.
  • Step 5 — Sign-off: at least two staff members must approve.

It takes 3–4 hours at regular daycare rates (half day up to 5 hours, or full day). Boarding dogs do it too. Pre-screens run Mondays, Tuesdays, Thursdays and Fridays with advance notice — call 480-207-1852.

Collars, harnesses, sweaters — what's appropriate?+

We require a plastic quick-release (snap-and-go) collar — buckle collars are dangerous in play groups because of their pointy edges. Harnesses come off during group play and boarding. Sweaters are allowed; it gets chilly in winter and we like everyone cozy.

Can my dog get sick around other dogs?+

For a healthy, well-adjusted dog on a quality diet, the risk is quite low — but exposure is possible. We require vaccines, keep high sanitation standards, use quality air scrubbers and solid-wall villas, sanitize indoors daily and clean outdoor potty yards twice daily. Dogs with separation anxiety or weaker immune systems are more susceptible — request extra rest during boarding if it would help.

Is my dog's food healthy?+

Diet affects health and immunity. Check how your dog's food is rated on DogFoodAdvisor — read the "Bottom Line," the star rating and any recalls.

What forms are required?+

A Client Profile, Pet Profile and Waiver & Release of Liability are required for boarding and daycare. Start with new client sign-up →

🐾 Our mission

We love & care for your dog.

We're here to serve busy pet owners and want to be convenient on your route to work, travel or activities. Through our many years of pet care, we've learned how to communicate, observe, and respond to different types of dog needs.

Our mission is to improve every aspect of your pet boarding experience — to be a place where pet owners feel welcome and trust that their loving babies are safe and cared for by trained staff who want the best for all animals.

With four play yards and climate-controlled indoor and outdoor playgrounds, we pair similar-size dogs together. Teacup dogs play with other dainty petite dogs 10 lb and under — they need more human touch to feel reassured and safe, and we take extra precautions for them.

💜 Linda & Maverick
🐾 Why I'm in the pet-care business

One puppy changed everything.

An abandoned puppy named Ria

Our founder was in her 40s when she got her first dog. Living in Del Rio, Texas, she stopped at her vet's office for cat food and learned someone had abandoned a Black Lab–Border Collie mix puppy there. It was Friday, the office would be closed all weekend, and she couldn't bear the puppy being alone — so she took her home "just for the weekend." Already the mom of nine cats (possibly more!), she kept the puppy and named her Rio, later Ria.

Looking for something better

After moving to Bentonville, Arkansas, and traveling to Asia for work several times a year, she tried three boarding facilities near home. Each time something felt a little off, and she never went back to the same one twice. She wanted a place where Ria would be safe, understood, treated with compassion and genuinely cared for — and knew she couldn't be the only dog parent searching.

A blunt conversation

At a PetSmart adoption event, a rescue volunteer heard her boarding idea and said, bluntly, "another idiot who doesn't know what they're doing." Harsh — but honest. Loving dogs wasn't enough on its own. So she learned: in 2007 she trained with a top dog-care franchise and built an indoor-outdoor facility from the ground up in Bentonville, opening in August 2008.

  • 2008First indoor-outdoor dog-care facility opens in Bentonville, Arkansas — it still operates today under new ownership.
  • Aug 22, 2014Happy Pets Palace & Playground opens in Mesa — independent, so every decision is about what's right for dogs, families and our team.
  • Dec 12, 2018Chandler opens on Amber's birthday — she was instrumental in launching it and still leads it.
  • TodaySix years running as Mesa's Best Pet Boarding, with a whole pack of happy regulars.

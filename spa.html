🐾 How it works

Clean pup, no effort.

Our spa runs alongside daycare and boarding rather than as a standalone salon. If your dog is in for the day or staying the night, add a bath, brush-out, de-shed, nail trim or facial to that stay.

We use the Rockin' Paws shampoo line — made in the USA, pH-balanced, sulfate-free and paraben-free — to gently remove dirt, neutralize odors and support a healthy skin barrier. Both locations offer the same services at the same prices.

Add it when you reserve the stay, or call 480-207-1852 if your dog is already booked.

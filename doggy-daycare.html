🐾 Why daycare?

A tired dog is a happy dog.

Our dog daycare offers up to 12 hours a day of supervised dog-to-dog group play, Monday through Friday, with limited Sunday daycare (please call ahead). If you have a high-energy dog you'd like tired out when you get home from work — or a pup who simply loves canine company — daycare is the answer.

Your dog gets hours of attention from our trained, loving staff and play with dogs that closely match their size and play style. Once your dog has passed pre-screening, no reservation is needed for regular weekday daycare — drop off whenever it suits you.

🐾 Safe play

The right playmates.

We have 3 separate indoor/outdoor playgrounds so every dog fits their group's size, energy and comfort. Our PackPro-trained staff also consider temperament, confidence, age and play style — and every group is actively supervised all day.

Our smallest guests have a space all their own. We're also one of the few facilities that welcome bully breeds, who must be on their best behavior to stay in group play.

Timing tips

  • High-energy dogs do best dropped off by 9 AM, while the group is still raring to go.
  • Around 11 AM the morning excitement settles; energy picks up again later in the afternoon.
  • Half-day dogs must be picked up by 3 PM, so arrive by 10 AM for the full five hours.

🐾 Requirements

Before the first day.

📋

Pre-screening

Every dog does a friendly evaluation on their first day (3–4 hours, normal daycare rates) to make sure group play is a good fit. How it works →

💉

Vaccines

Current rabies and distemper from a licensed vet. Puppies need one set of vaccines at least 7 days before day one, plus a clear fecal test under 6 months.

✂️

Spay / neuter

Required by 2 years of age. Dogs must also be free of fleas, ticks and parasites like ear mites.

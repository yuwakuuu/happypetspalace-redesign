🐾 What sets us apart

Why owners choose the Palace.

Six years running we've been voted Best Pet Boarding Services in Mesa, and our Google reviews say much the same. It comes down to a few choices we made early and haven't moved off since.

❤️‍🩹

Trained staff on every floor

Everyone is trained in pet first aid and CPR and in reading canine body language — so they can tell good play from play about to tip over and redirect it before it does. Semi-annual training includes safety for Arizona heat.

🌙

The overnight, done right

Boarding dogs get an outdoor potty break at around 10 PM and a check by late-night staff. A house-trained dog shouldn't have to unlearn that while you travel.

You can watch

Live-stream webcams run at both locations. Add a Camera View Villa for 24/7 viewing of your dog's own villa — group play and the quiet hours in between.

⚖️

Grouped by size & temperament

Separate climate-controlled indoor/outdoor yards. Teacup dogs (10 lb and under) get their own space and extra human touch.

🧼

Clean, healthy, solid-wall villas

Indoor areas sanitized daily, outdoor yards cleaned twice daily, quality air scrubbers and reverse-osmosis water for boarders.

🐾 Requirements & FAQs

Good questions.

What vaccinations are required?+

All dogs must be current on rabies and distemper, administered and documented by a licensed veterinarian. We suggest puppies receive at least one Bordetella vaccination. We absolutely accept titers for distemper to help prevent over-vaccinating.

Is spay / neuter required?+

Yes — dogs must be spayed or neutered by 2 years of age. Female dogs can't be in season while at Happy Pets Palace. All dogs must be suitable for co-ed group play.

What is the pre-screening process?+

After a quick phone or in-person chat, your dog does a 5-step evaluation on their first day of daycare: meeting staff, leash introductions, joining a small group, pack feedback, and sign-off by two staff members. It takes 3–4 hours and regular daycare rates apply. Boarding dogs go through it too.

Pre-screens are Mondays, Tuesdays, Thursdays and Fridays with advance notice — call 480-207-1852.

Can we tour your facility?+

Yes! We LOVE to give tours. Stop by anytime we're open and we'll show you around — no appointment needed.

Collars, harnesses, sweaters — what's okay?+

We require a plastic quick-release (snap-and-go) collar. Buckle collars are dangerous in group play. Harnesses come off during group play and boarding. Sweaters are welcome — Arizona winters get chilly!

What time is boarding checkout?+

Checkout is 10:30 AM every day. Late checkout until 6:30 PM is $24. Check-in is as early as 6 AM weekdays and 8 AM weekends/holidays, with no early drop-off fee.

What about holidays?+

We're open 365 days for boarding pickup and drop-off. Holiday hours are 8:00–10:30 AM and 4:00–6:30 PM. A $20 convenience charge applies for pickup on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas.

Do you give medications? What about food?+

Medications are given at no extra charge. Please bring your dog's own food to keep their diet consistent — and pack an extra day's worth of food and meds in case travel plans change.

Can my dog get sick around other dogs?+

The risk is low for healthy dogs. We require vaccinations, sanitize indoors daily, clean outdoor yards twice daily, and use air scrubbers and solid-wall villa construction. You can request extra rest during boarding if your dog would benefit.

What if my dog has fleas or ticks?+

A bath and removal of fleas or ticks can cost up to $80 depending on severity and the dog's size.

What's your cancellation policy?+

For boarding, please cancel at least 48 hours before the first night.

Do you take bully breeds and small dogs?+

Yes — we're one of the few facilities that welcome bully breeds (on their best behavior in group play). Teacup dogs 10 lb and under have their own play space with extra human touch.

What forms are required?+

A Client Profile, Pet Profile and Waiver & Release of Liability are required for boarding and daycare. Start with our new client sign-up.

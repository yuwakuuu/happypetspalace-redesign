🐾 What is it?

Play with a purpose.

Our Enrichment Center offers fun activities that instill positive behaviors, confidence and good manners through focused training with basic commands.

We set up an obstacle course with wobble boards and inflatable balance peanuts that build confidence and trust on unfamiliar surfaces, then move into nose work, puzzles and training games that reward focus. There's a doggy treadmill at Chandler, too.

Sessions work all five senses — touch, smell, taste, hearing and sight — for a workout that's mental and physical. Groups of 3–5 pups help puppies learn manners around other dogs and how to wait their turn.

🐶 Great for puppies

Puppies up to 6 months benefit hugely from practising routine commands in a busier environment — repetition is key. Two smaller sessions with other pups teach manners in a small-group setting.

Dogs who love it.

Puppies

Learn to read social cues from other dogs in a clean, fun and safe environment.

🧠

Clever dogs

Highly intelligent dogs who get bored quickly and do better when their intellect is challenged.

⚡

High-drive dogs

Dogs born with drive need mental and physical work so they can relax when they get home.

🌱

New socializers

Adults new to socialization who show a big interest in other dogs.

🫣

Shy pups

Dogs who prefer people — a group of 3–5 is far easier to read than a full play yard.

🧘

Low-tolerance adults

Adults who love fun but have a shorter tolerance for larger groups.

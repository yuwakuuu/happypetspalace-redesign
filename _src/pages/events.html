Each Saturday is split into groups so dogs play with dogs their own size and age — that split is the whole point. A confident 12-pound terrier and a four-month-old Bernese puppy both have a much better morning when they're not in the same pack. These are community gatherings, not drop-off: you stay with your dog for the whole session.

🐕‍🦺8:00 – 9:30 AM

Toy Breeds

Toy dogs and puppies of every age up to 20 lb — Chihuahuas, Yorkies, Maltese, small poodles, dachshunds and the rest of the tiny warriors. Games, prizes and photos.

🏆10:00 – 11:30 AM

Featured Breed Groups

A different breed group each week, so you meet the people who chose the same breed you did.

🍼12:00 – 1:30 PM

Large-Breed Puppy Social

Large-breed puppies up to six months, during the window when socializing does the most good. Training tips, games, prizes and photos.

Nov 21 · Arctic breedsNov 28 · Labs & Lab mixesDec 5 · German Shepherds & BelgiansDec 12 · Goldens, English Creams & retriever mixes
🐾 Who can come

Bring your buddy.

  • Toy Breed Social: friendly toy breed dogs and puppies up to 20 lb, current on vaccinations.
  • Large-Breed Puppy Social: friendly large-breed puppies up to six months, current on age-appropriate vaccinations, with a negative fecal test.
  • Featured breed groups: dogs of that week's breed group, current on vaccinations.

Everyone follows City of Apache Junction dog park rules. Cost is free — please bring water and treats for your dog. The exact address and your group's start time are on the calendar entry for each date.

Questions? Call 480-207-1852 and ask for extension 2 — we'll tell you which group your dog fits and what to bring.

🐾 More than loving animals

Screens off, paws on.

Pet Professional Academy is a hands-on educational and career-exploration program that helps young animal lovers turn their passion into practical skills. Through supervised experiences in dog behavior, safe handling, training, bathing, enrichment, pet photography and customer service, students discover what working professionally with pets is really like.

Along the way they step away from their screens, connect with people and animals, and strengthen the confidence, communication, responsibility and teamwork they'll need for their first job and beyond.

Eligibility: ages 12–16, or students who have completed 5th grade. Younger students may qualify through our Younger Student Admission Process. Classes are capped at 8 students at both Mesa and Chandler.

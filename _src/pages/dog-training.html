🐾 How we teach

Reward the good stuff.

We use positive reinforcement, reward-based training: we reward the behaviors we like, and gently redirect and teach alternatives for the ones we don't.

We run three options, meant to be read as a sequence: S.T.A.R. Puppy is the foundation, Canine Good Citizen is the progression, and Play & Train is the drop-off option folded into a day your dog is already here. The AKC courses run with an independent AKC-certified trainer at both Mesa and Chandler.

🐾 The programs

Pick your path.

⭐

AKC S.T.A.R. Puppy

Foundation. Puppies under 1 year just starting out. Six consecutive weeks, one hour a week, with you in the room — live demos, guided practice and time for questions. Builds confident, well-mannered puppies and a stronger bond.

🏅

AKC Canine Good Citizen

Progression. Dogs with the basics in place. Six weeks ending in the CGC test and an AKC-recognized title — genuinely useful for rental housing and travel. The evaluation alone is $25, but the course is strongly recommended.

🎾

Play & Train

Drop-off. One-on-one AKC skill reinforcement during a daycare or boarding day — no extra appointments. Best for dogs who know the skills but lose them under pressure. Available as a daycare or boarding add-on.

🧭 Short version: under a year and starting out → S.T.A.R. Puppy. Basics in place and you want the title → Canine Good Citizen. No room in your week → Play & Train.
🐾 Inside a six-week course

Sit, even with distractions.

Across six weeks we move from single skills in a calm room toward the same skills under distraction — the part that actually matters. A dog who sits in an empty room has not learned to sit. A dog who sits while another dog walks past has.

Plan on short daily practice between classes — a few minutes at a time beats one long session, especially for puppies. The final week is the evaluation.

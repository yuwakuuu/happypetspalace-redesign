🐾 Built around their routine

Home routine, palace stay.

Overnight boarding here is built around one idea: your dog should come home on the same routine they left on. That means real outdoor yards rather than an indoor run, a late potty break at around 10 PM so nobody has to hold it until morning, and meals from the food you packed.

Boarding runs at both Mesa and Chandler, every day of the year, holidays included. It suits dogs who are comfortable in group play and have passed our pre-screening. New to us? Book the pre-screening first — it runs like a half day of daycare and lets us place your dog in the right playgroup on arrival day.

🐾 Before you board

Good things to know.

Hours, check-in & holidays

Open 365 days a year for boarding pickup and drop-off. Check-in from 6 AM weekdays and 8 AM weekends & holidays, with no early drop-off fee. A $20 lobby fee applies to pickup on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas (holiday hours 8–10:30 AM and 4–6:30 PM).

Discounts

5% off 11+ consecutive nights. 10% U.S. Military discount with government ID on boarding and full-price daycare (not baths, add-ons, afternoon checkouts or packages). Discounts can't be combined.

Health requirements

Current rabies and distemper, spayed/neutered by 2 years old, and flea & tick free. Every dog must pass pre-screening and keep good standing. Away for two months or more? We recommend a daycare day before boarding.

Payment & cancellation

Please cancel at least 48 hours before the first night, or one night's stay is charged. We accept cash, check, Visa, American Express, Mastercard & Discover.

🐾 Planning your stay

Book early, pack smart.

📅

Reserve early

Summer, fall and spring breaks, Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's and three-day weekends fill fast. Book as soon as your travel dates are set.

🎒

What to pack

Their regular food for the whole stay plus a day extra, labeled feeding notes, meds and treats. We provide bowls, raised beds and fleece blankets — bring a favorite toy or something that smells like you.

📋

Pre-screen first

A first visit lets your dog learn our team and yards, and lets us pick the right playgroup. Do it before busy holidays so arrival day is easy.

🐾 Boarding FAQs

Boarding questions.

Do I need to bring my dog's food?+

Yes — and pack one extra day in case plans change. We have a fridge, freezer and microwave and will prepare meals the way you do at home. If food runs out, we'll buy the brand your dog eats (a $5 trip charge applies).

Can my dogs stay together?+

Yes. Dogs from the same household can eat and sleep together, or eat separately and still sleep together. With a new family dog, we suggest separate villas until you're sure — they'll likely still share a playgroup.

Is someone there all night?+

There's no overnight staff, but a team member comes in at 10 PM to check every dog and give each boarder a late potty break at no charge. Both locations have monitored security and 24/7 webcams.

What if there's an after-hours emergency?+

Emergency veterinarians are within a few miles of both locations.

Do you give medications or insulin?+

Yes, at no extra charge, following your vet's routine. Insulin times need to fall within our hours of operation.

Is my dog too old for boarding?+

We welcome dogs at every stage of life, and seniors often perk up around their friends. If a senior has trouble standing, is incontinent, anxious or in poor health, we recommend at-home sitting or boarding with a vet.

What's the youngest age for boarding?+

See the puppy health and vaccination requirements on our Puppy Socializing page.

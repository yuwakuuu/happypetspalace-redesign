Portrait Studio

Because your pet is family, and joy deserves to be beautifully captured forever.

Book a Chandler session →📞 Call for Mesa
📸 Edited digital portraits included
🐾 Professional pet photography

Say cheese!

Our studio sits inside Happy Pets Palace, so your dog is photographed somewhere they already know, by people they already know — no strange studio, no long drive. Sessions run from a three-minute keepsake shot while your dog is already in for the day, up to a full hour with four backdrops and a short video.

A pet must be in every photo — family, friends and groups are welcome to join. Backdrops change through the year.
